# Community Market Strategy and Location Intelligence Study

## Project Overview

This project uses demographic, geographic, and market data to evaluate community growth opportunities and support site-selection decisions.

The analysis demonstrates how location intelligence can be used to identify target populations, understand market conditions, compare potential locations, and develop practical recommendations.

## Business Question

Which locations offer the strongest combination of population demand, household characteristics, accessibility, and long-term growth potential?

## Analysis Performed

The study considers:

- Population size and growth
- Age distribution
- Household composition
- Income and affordability
- Education levels
- Employment characteristics
- Housing conditions
- Travel distance and accessibility
- Competitive presence
- Geographic market coverage

## Analytical Process

1. Defined the target population and market objective.
2. Identified potential geographic areas.
3. Collected demographic and economic data.
4. Compared population and household characteristics.
5. Evaluated accessibility and surrounding market conditions.
6. Mapped the strongest potential locations.
7. Developed a location and market strategy recommendation.
