# Senior Living Market Feasibility and Investment Study

## Project Overview

This project evaluates the feasibility of a senior living investment using demographic trends, market demand, competitive supply, real estate fundamentals, and financial analysis.

The purpose of the study is to determine whether a selected market can support a new senior living community and identify the factors that would influence investment performance.

## Business Question

Does the target market demonstrate sufficient demographic demand, competitive positioning, and financial potential to support a senior living investment?

## Analysis Performed

The study considers:

- Population and household growth
- Senior population trends
- Income and affordability
- Existing senior living communities
- Competitive supply
- Occupancy and market demand
- Site and location characteristics
- Development and operating assumptions
- Revenue and expense projections
- Investment risk and opportunity

## Analytical Process

1. Defined the target market and geographic study area.
2. Collected demographic and real estate market data.
3. Evaluated the existing competitive supply.
4. Estimated potential demand for senior housing.
5. Reviewed financial and operating assumptions.
6. Identified major investment risks.
7. Developed a market-based investment recommendation.
